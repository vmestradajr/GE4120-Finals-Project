# Activity 3: Infographic Design

## Description
This activity involved creating clear, organized, and visually appealing infographics while ensuring that the information presented is understandable and informative.

## What Have I Learned?
Through this activity, I developed my skills in creating clear, organized, and visually appealing infographics while making sure the information is easy to understand. One challenge I encountered was finding reliable references and resources to use as the basis for my infographic. This experience taught me the importance of gathering credible information and presenting it effectively. I can improve by practicing more, exploring different infographic designs, and learning how to organize information in a clear and effective manner.
