# Activity 2: Brand Design

## Description
This activity focused on combining colors and design elements to create an attractive, unique, and memorable brand.

## What Have I Learned?
Through this activity, I learned how to combine colors and design elements to create an attractive and memorable brand. One challenge I faced was deciding what kind of brand to create and how to make it unique and appealing. This experience helped me understand the importance of creativity and consistency in developing a brand identity. In the future, I can apply this knowledge by carefully choosing appropriate color palettes and font styles when creating a brand or other designs.
