# Activity 7: Website Customization

## Description
This activity focused on further developing website creation skills, particularly in customizing website content, improving its layout, and making its overall presentation more engaging.

## What Have I Learned?
Through this activity, I further developed my skills in creating and customizing websites, especially in organizing content and improving the overall presentation. One challenge I faced was deciding how to improve my website and what additional content or features would make it more useful and engaging. This experience helped me recognize the importance of continuous improvement and thoughtful website design. In the future, I can apply this knowledge to academic projects, personal portfolios, and other opportunities where presenting information online is useful.
